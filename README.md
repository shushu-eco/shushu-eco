## `03` Link Lite Recorder

<img align="right" width="96" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shushu-eco/linkturbo-site/main/readme/logo.png" alt="Link logo">

**A browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Brave.** It records the Kick or Twitch stream playing in your tab, lets you mark the highlights as they happen, and saves one mp4 to your own computer.

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-available_now-1374a0?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a0a0a)
![Free](https://img.shields.io/badge/free-open_source-1374a0?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a0a0a)
![Licence](https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-GPL--3.0-1374a0?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a0a0a)

<br clear="right">

<div align="center">
<a href="https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/link-lite-recorder/jjbnifoddmcjocjdmiaondcimkjmkjaa"><img src="assets/demo.gif" width="800" alt="Link Lite on a stream's page: Record, a mark with a note, Stop, and the mp4 that is saved"></a>
</div>

<br>

| | |
|---|---|
| **● Record** | Picture and sound, cut out of the page. One mp4, up to 30 minutes. |
| **★ Mark** | A hotkey and a few words ("clutch 1v3"). Or by itself, when chat speeds up. |
| **⟲ Rewind** | Switched on, it keeps the last 3 minutes, so a moment can be saved after it happened. |
| **▣ For your editor** | The marks come with the recording: a list to copy, or a marker file for DaVinci Resolve. |
| **⌂ Stays on your computer** | Nothing is uploaded. No account, no analytics. |

<div align="center">
<img src="assets/marks.gif" width="800" alt="After the stream: a recording's marks, Copy list, and Markers for Resolve">
<br><br>
<img src="assets/rewind.gif" width="800" alt="The rewind keeps the last minutes; Save last makes an mp4 of the minute before">
</div>

## `04` Link TURBO

**The full program for Windows: from a live stream to a posted reel, on one computer.**

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-available_now-1374a0?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a0a0a)
![Free](https://img.shields.io/badge/free-open_source-1374a0?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a0a0a)
![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Windows-10_%2F_11-1374a0?style=flat-square&labelColor=0a0a0a)

<div align="center">
<a href="https://linkturbo.app/turbo"><img src="assets/turbo.gif" width="800" alt="Link TURBO: from a live stream to a posted reel, in five steps that light up one after the other"></a>
</div>

<br>

| | |
|---|---|
| **01 · Record** | **Studio.** Up to 3 Kick or Twitch streams recorded at once. |
| **02 · Find** | **Moments found for you.** Up to 30 ranked moments per recording. |
| **03 · Edit** | **Finished 9:16 clips.** Title, hook and captions timed to the voice. |
| **04 · Post** | **YouTube Shorts and Instagram.** Only the clips you choose leave your computer. |
| **05 · Measure** | **Analytics.** See how each clip did: 7 days, 28 days or all time. |
| **06 · Night** | **Link Night.** Records your streams while you sleep and has the moments ranked when you are back. |

<div align="center">
<a href="https://linkturbo.app/turbo"><img src="assets/parts.gif" width="800" alt="The parts of Link TURBO that work for you: Analytics with its numbers, Link Night recording through the night, and the Discord remote answering from your phone"></a>
</div>

It keeps itself up to date. The code is in [shushu-eco/link](https://github.com/shushu-eco/link).
